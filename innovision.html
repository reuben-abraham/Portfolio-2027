CASE STUDY
Identity Design

Embarking on a Space Odyssey: Innovision

Branding direction, typography, and poster graphics designed for Eastern India's largest techno-management festival.

Role Lead Branding Designer (Team Lead)
Team 4 Designers & Illustrators
Platform Print (Banners, IDs), Web, Video
Date October 2019
01 / Concept

A Space Opera

Innovision is the largest techno-management fest of Eastern India, gathering over 10,000 students. Each year features a distinct theme around which decoration, marketing, and events are aligned.

For the 2019 edition, the theme was "A Space Opera". This theme opened up a wide range of assets, illustrations, and galactic concepts that we could weave into posters, banners, and digital platforms.

02 / Style Code

Style Guide & Visual Assets

To create the promotional materials, the team established a dark, immersive style code. We utilized high-resolution celestial photos, deep space backgrounds, and glowing stars to convey a futuristic feel.

Typography

We searched for typefaces representing a galactic theme while preserving legibility. We chose "Equinox" for display titles and "PF Din" for compact, modern body copy.

03 / Logomark

Logomark & Stationery

The Innovision symbol had poor brand recall, so we designed a wordmark to supplement it. This unified mark was utilized on ID badges, certificates, folders, and letterheads to establish a coherent brand identity.

04 / Collaterals

Posters & Promos

The final designs maintained a high degree of visual coherence, helping to drive immediate interest and public engagement. We also directed a promotional teaser video mapping preceding festival clips to the space theme.
