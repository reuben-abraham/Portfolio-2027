2020 · iOS concept · with Saumyaa Suneja

Barter: borrowing from the people next door

Why buy something you need for a week, when someone on a campus of 6,000 already owns it? Barter was a peer-to-peer lending app for NIT Rourkela, designed in a two-week sprint with Saumyaa Suneja. I worked on research, visuals and interaction flows.

A campus survey and interviews split people into borrowers, worried about finding things and trusting strangers, and lenders, worried about damage and late returns. Trust became the system: a temporary security deposit, wear-and-tear listed up front, video handovers, sign-in with a campus roll number, and a reciprocity rule. After your first borrow, you list three items to keep going.

2019 · Identity · led a team of 4

Innovision 2019: a space opera

Innovision is Eastern India's largest techno-management festival, with more than 10,000 students attending. The 2019 theme was "A Space Opera". I led a team of four designers and illustrators on the identity, posters, stationery and a teaser video.

The style was dark and immersive: deep-space photography, glowing stars, Equinox for display and PF Din for compact body copy. The old symbol had poor recall, so we designed a wordmark to sit beside it on everything from ID badges to letterheads.

2018 · Identity · solo, with conference leads

International MUN: formal, but young

After the first Model United Nations at NIT Rourkela went well, the second edition aimed to go international. It needed an identity that looked credible to delegates and sponsors without feeling stiff.

I built a system of smooth gradients, maps and flags, set in Rajdhani for its geometric, structured letters. The logomark carried across badges, booklets and stationery, and a poster series tied together by one colour story ran the registration campaign.
