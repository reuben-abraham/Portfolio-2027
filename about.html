About Reuben

Hi, I'm Reuben. I'm an interaction and product designer focusing on digital tools and high-fidelity systems that feel as satisfying as a well-bound sketchbook. I believe that digital interfaces should be responsive, tactile, and designed with human physics in mind.

Over the past years, I've had the privilege of designing premium digital experiences for millions of users at Grab and Disney+ Hotstar. From reshaping in-store restaurant dining systems to scaling advanced booking interfaces and streaming video discovery, my work bridges the gap between complex engineering architectures and visual elegance.

Currently based in Singapore, I split my time between writing clean frontend code, refining micro-interactions, and collecting tiny interface rituals that make software feel less flat.
