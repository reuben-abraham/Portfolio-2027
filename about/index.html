Hi, I'm Reuben. I like the moment just before someone says yes.

I'm a product designer in Singapore. At Grab I've worked on transport and dining: airport pickups that follow your flight, and a QR menu a whole table can order from. Before that, at Disney+ Hotstar, I worked on how millions of people find something to watch, sign in on their TV, and get help when something breaks.

Most of my work sits where a product asks someone to commit: press play, book the ride, place the order. I like those moments because they're measurable, and because the fix is rarely a new feature. More often it's taking a step away.

I start with data and conversations in roughly equal measure: funnels, cold calls, field visits, card sorts. I prototype early, sometimes in Figma and sometimes in code, and I like being in the room with engineering when the trade-offs get made.

I also write front-end code. This site is hand-written HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript. When I'm not designing, I'm laughing at lame jokes or baking cheesecake. Type either word on any page.
