CASE STUDY
UX Case Study

Transitioning to Subscriber-Only TV Service

From Freemium to Premium: Driving subscription growth on Living Room (LR) devices without impacting user retention.

Role Product Designer (User Journeys, Visual Assets, Interactions)
Team 1 Senior Designer, 1 Product Manager, Engineering
Platform Smart TV (Android TV, TizenOS, Apple TV, FireTV, Roku)
Date April 2021
01 / Context

Background & Hypothesis

Disney+ Hotstar is India's biggest streaming service with more than 28 million paid users (as of April 2021). The app is available across multiple living room (LR) platforms. Historically, the app functioned as a secondary node, depending on mobile or web for login and payments. However, these devices account for a significant share of watch time.

Initial Hypothesis

Users watching on LR devices like smart TVs can afford a subscription to Disney+ Hotstar, and making the app subscriber-only on these devices can increase acquisitions without hurting retention.

02 / Research

User Research

Validating through Data:

  • Over 4 times as many users are on active subscriptions on LR devices compared to Android & iOS mobile devices.
  • Majority of free account users on TV watch live news and free daily serials.

User Interviews:

We talked to free account users on LR devices to understand what prevented them from subscribing to a paid plan. Key findings are shown in the cards below:

Identified Problems

  • Complicated and incoherent login flow.
  • Failure to surface relevant value propositions to drive subscription.
  • Broken flow for plan selection and payment via TV.
03 / Login UX

Frictionless Login

Status Quo Login: Users were required to open a web link on their phone, sign in, and enter a code shown on the TV.

New OTP Login Flow

Introduced direct phone number login. Users can enter their phone number on the TV screen, receive an OTP on their mobile, and log in instantly without opening websites. Email/Facebook login remains as a fallback.

04 / Landing UX

Communicating Value

Status Quo Landing Page: A generic page explaining how to log in was displayed, offering no emotional connection or plan comparison.

New Landing & Plan Selection

Implemented enticing show cards, clear subscription benefits, and a high-contrast side-by-side plan comparison page.

05 / Payments

Frictionless Payment

Status Quo Payments: Smart TV payments were not supported; users had to drop off and navigate manually to mobile or web checkout.

Seamless Checkout Experience

Users can select their desired plan directly on TV and transition to checkout instantly. They can choose to receive a payment link via SMS to complete payment on mobile, or scan the UPI QR code on the TV screen via any UPI app.
